About KhmerCraft Seller

A marketplace built with Cambodia’s makers, not just for them.

We believe the people who weave, cook, carve, grow and design in this country deserve tools as good as their work — simple enough to use between everything else, and fair enough to trust.

Our story

It started with a simple question.

Why is it so hard for a talented maker in Takeo or Kampong Chhnang to sell to someone in Phnom Penh — or across the country?

The products were never the problem. The problem was everything around them: being found, showing what you make, taking orders without losing details in a chat, knowing what is in stock, and getting paid without chasing screenshots.

KhmerCraft Seller is our answer. A storefront that is yours, inside a marketplace that buyers already visit, with a workspace that handles the admin so you can get back to the work you love.

Two sites, one account

A home for buyers. A home for sellers.

KhmerCraft is split in two on purpose. Buyers shop on the marketplace; you run your business here, on the seller portal. The same account works on both — so you can still shop, and see your store exactly as buyers do.
