Why we built this

Selling in Cambodia has always been personal. Online, it should stay that way.

Most of the best things made in this country are sold the same way they always have been — at a market stall, from a workshop door, or through a long chat thread full of photos, prices and screenshots of bank transfers. It works, but it does not grow. Every new buyer has to be found one message at a time.

KhmerCraft Seller gives that same personal business a proper home: a storefront with your name on it, inside a marketplace people already browse. Buyers find you through search and categories instead of needing your link. Orders arrive complete. Stock keeps itself counted.

You still decide everything — what to sell, what to charge, how your store looks and what your story says. We just take care of the parts that used to live in a notebook.

— The KhmerCraft team
